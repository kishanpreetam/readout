Readouts and cash runway

The screen joins each SEC-linked company's Phase 3 readouts with its runway: cash, equivalents and short-term investments divided by the average quarterly cash burn over the trailing 12 months, times 3. Quarterly burn comes from the operating cash flow line, derived from year-to-date XBRL values.

No rows in this run: SEC data was refused (see above). The logic is covered by tests/test_dbt_financials.py, which runs the dbt project on a small synthetic company and checks the derived quarters, the runway months and the funding-risk flag.

Every wrong answer

How many distinct active Phase 3 or Phase 2/3 studies have a primary completion date in the 12 months after the 2026-11-03 snapshot (on or after 2026-11-03 and before 2027-11-03)?

Prompt: schema_only. Got 1161 (1 row); gold 1003 (1 row).

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT nct_id) AS n_studies FROM marts.dim_trial WHERE is_active AND phase_number >= 2.5 AND primary_completion_date >= DATE '2026-11-03' AND primary_completion_date < DATE '2027-11-03'

List the NCT IDs of Amgen's active Phase 3 or Phase 2/3 studies (any role) with a primary completion date in the 12 months after 2026-11-03.

Prompt: schema_only. Got nct03969329 (18 rows); gold nct07614776 (15 rows).

SELECT DISTINCT t.nct_id
FROM marts.dim_trial t
JOIN marts.bridge_trial_organization b ON b.nct_id = t.nct_id
JOIN marts.dim_organization o ON o.org_id = b.org_id
WHERE o.org_name ILIKE '%amgen%'
  AND t.is_active
  AND t.phase_number >= 2.5
  AND t.primary_completion_date > DATE '2026-11-03'
  AND t.primary_completion_date <= DATE '2027-11-03'
ORDER BY t.nct_id

Which organizations have more than 20 upcoming active Phase 3 or Phase 2/3 readouts in the 12 months after 2026-11-03 (any role)? List their names.

Prompt: schema_only. Got astrazeneca (5 rows); gold astrazeneca (3 rows).

SELECT o.org_name, COUNT(DISTINCT t.nct_id) AS n_readouts
FROM marts.bridge_trial_organization b
JOIN marts.dim_trial t ON t.nct_id = b.nct_id
JOIN marts.dim_organization o ON o.org_id = b.org_id
WHERE t.is_active
  AND t.phase_number >= 2.5
  AND t.primary_completion_date > DATE '2026-11-03'
  AND t.primary_completion_date <= DATE '2026-11-03' + INTERVAL 12 MONTH
GROUP BY o.org_name
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT t.nct_id) > 20
ORDER BY n_readouts DESC

Hand review: every miss has the same cause. Without documented phase codes the model filtered phase_number >= 2.5 for “Phase 3 or Phase 2/3”, which also counts Phase 4. The documented prompt answered all of these correctly.
